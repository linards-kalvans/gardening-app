# Business Requirements Document
## Gardening App for Northern European Hobbyists

### 1. Target Audience & Market
- **Primary Users**: Hobbyist gardeners with small backyard gardens in Northern Europe/Baltic region
- **Technical Level**: Basic smartphone users, prefer simple interfaces
- **Platform**: Mobile-friendly web application (responsive design)

### 2. Core Business Objectives
- Help users plan and manage their gardens effectively
- Provide climate-appropriate plant suggestions for Northern European conditions
- Enable tracking of plant locations and care schedules
- Support both planning-first and plant-first user workflows
- Promote native plants while accommodating climate change opportunities

### 3. Functional Requirements

#### 3.1 Garden Planning & Management
- **Visual Garden Mapping**: Users can create and edit garden layouts
- **Photo-to-Map Conversion**: Upload garden photos that can be transformed into interactive maps
- **Plant Placement Tracking**: Remember what is planted where with visual indicators
- **Multiple Garden Support**: (Future consideration, not MVP requirement)

#### 3.2 Plant Database & Recommendations
- **Comprehensive Edible Plant Database**: Extensive database focusing on edible plants suitable for Northern Europe
- **Climate-Appropriate Suggestions**: Recommend plants based on Northern European/Baltic climate
- **Native Plant Promotion**: Highlight and promote native plants among common varieties
- **Climate Change Adaptation**: Include experimental planting features with climate risk warnings
- **Companion Planting**: Suggest beneficial plant combinations
- **Spacing & Compatibility**: Help with proper plant spacing and compatibility

#### 3.3 Crop Rotation & Planning
- **Rotation History Tracking**: Track up to 3-4 years of planting history
- **Rotation Suggestions**: Recommend optimal rotation patterns based on plant families
- **Multiple Options**: Always provide several rotation options following best practices

#### 3.4 Reminder & Care System
- **Comprehensive Reminders**: Watering, fertilizing, pruning, harvesting, planting dates, frost protection
- **User Customizable**: Users can select which reminders they want to receive
- **Multiple Notification Methods**: Push notifications, calendar integration, simple checklists
- **Default Settings**: All reminders enabled by default

#### 3.5 User Onboarding & Personalization
- **Simple Setup Process**: Guide inexperienced users through basic questions
- **Location-Based Features**: City input with optional GPS for local weather/frost dates
- **Garden Characteristics**: Collect garden size, soil type, sun exposure through user-friendly questions
- **No Technical Expertise Required**: Avoid requiring soil analysis or technical knowledge

#### 3.6 Data Management
- **Cloud Storage**: All data stored in cloud for accessibility and backup
- **Cross-Device Sync**: Access garden data from any device
- **Data Persistence**: Maintain garden history and plant records

### 4. Technical Requirements
- **Platform**: Mobile-friendly web application
- **Responsive Design**: Optimized for smartphone use
- **Simple Interface**: Straightforward, intuitive user experience
- **Cloud Infrastructure**: Reliable cloud storage and sync capabilities

### 5. Business Model & Launch Strategy
- **Initial Testing**: Personal use and validation
- **Growth Strategy**: Social media promotion after personal validation
- **Success Metrics**: Personal effectiveness and user satisfaction
- **Geographic Focus**: Northern Europe/Baltic region initially

### 6. Key Success Factors
- **Climate Relevance**: Must be specifically tailored to Northern European growing conditions
- **User Simplicity**: Easy for beginners to use without technical gardening knowledge
- **Comprehensive Coverage**: Address all aspects of garden planning and maintenance
- **Flexible Workflows**: Support both planning-first and plant-first approaches

### 7. Future Considerations (Post-MVP)
- Multiple garden support
- Advanced analytics and insights
- Community features
- Integration with local nurseries or gardening centers
