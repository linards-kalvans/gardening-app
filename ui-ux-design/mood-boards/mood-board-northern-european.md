### Typography Inspiration

#### Primary Fonts (Mobile-Optimized)
- **Headings**: Inter Bold (clean, modern, highly readable)
- **Body Text**: Source Sans Pro Regular (excellent mobile readability)
- **UI Elements**: Inter Medium (consistent with headings)

#### Font Hierarchy
- **H1**: 32px, Inter Bold, Line Height 1.2
- **H2**: 24px, Inter Bold, Line Height 1.3
- **H3**: 20px, Inter Medium, Line Height 1.4
- **Body**: 16px, Source Sans Pro Regular, Line Height 1.5
- **Small**: 14px, Source Sans Pro Regular, Line Height 1.4

### Visual Elements

#### Natural Textures
- **Wood Grain**: Subtle background textures for cards
- **Stone**: Rough, natural stone patterns for dividers
- **Soil**: Earthy textures for plant-related sections
- **Water**: Subtle wave patterns for irrigation features

#### Organic Shapes
- **Rounded Corners**: 8px radius for buttons, 12px for cards
- **Flowing Lines**: Curved dividers and section breaks
- **Natural Icons**: Hand-drawn style for plant and garden icons
- **Organic Layouts**: Asymmetric but balanced compositions

### Seasonal Visual Cues

#### Spring
- Fresh green gradients
- Budding plant imagery
- Soft, diffused lighting
- Renewal and growth themes

#### Summer
- Deep, rich greens
- Vibrant plant colors
- Strong sunlight effects
- Abundance and harvest themes

#### Autumn
- Warm earth tones
- Falling leaves patterns
- Golden hour lighting
- Harvest and preparation themes

#### Winter
- Cool, muted tones
- Frost and snow effects
- Evergreen accents
- Rest and planning themes

### Mobile-First Design Patterns

#### Touch-Friendly Elements
- **Minimum Touch Target**: 44px x 44px
- **Button Spacing**: 16px between interactive elements
- **Thumb Zone**: Primary actions in bottom 1/3 of screen
- **Swipe Gestures**: Natural navigation patterns

#### Authentication UI Patterns
- **OAuth Buttons**: Full-width, prominent placement
- **Loading States**: Subtle spinners with progress text
- **Error States**: Clear, actionable error messages
- **Success States**: Smooth transitions with confirmation

### Accessibility Considerations

#### Color Contrast
- **Primary Text**: 4.5:1 contrast ratio minimum
- **Secondary Text**: 3:1 contrast ratio minimum
- **Interactive Elements**: 3:1 contrast ratio minimum
- **Focus Indicators**: 2:1 contrast ratio minimum

#### Typography Accessibility
- **Font Size**: Minimum 16px for body text
- **Line Height**: 1.5x font size for readability
- **Letter Spacing**: 0.1em for small text
- **Word Spacing**: 0.16em for optimal reading

### Northern European Cultural Elements

#### Design Philosophy
- **Minimalism**: Clean, uncluttered interfaces
- **Functionality**: Form follows function
- **Sustainability**: Eco-friendly design choices
- **Community**: Social aspects of gardening

#### Visual Language
- **Natural Materials**: Wood, stone, metal textures
- **Seasonal Awareness**: Weather-appropriate imagery
- **Local Identity**: Regional plant and garden references
- **Practical Beauty**: Aesthetic that serves function

### Component Design Guidelines

#### Buttons
- **Primary**: Forest green background, white text
- **Secondary**: White background, forest green border
- **OAuth**: Brand colors (Google red, Facebook blue)
- **Size**: Minimum 44px height, 8px border radius

#### Cards
- **Background**: White with subtle shadow
- **Border**: 1px solid #E5E5E5
- **Radius**: 12px for organic feel
- **Padding**: 16px internal spacing

#### Input Fields
- **Border**: 2px solid #E5E5E5, 4px solid on focus
- **Focus Color**: Forest green (#2D5016)
- **Error Color**: Rust red (#B7410E)
- **Success Color**: Sage green (#9CAF88)

---

*This mood board captures the essence of Northern European gardening culture while ensuring the design system supports excellent usability and accessibility for our target audience of hobbyist gardeners.*
