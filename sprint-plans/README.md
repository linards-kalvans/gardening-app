## Sprint Plan Structure

Each sprint plan includes:
- **Sprint Overview**: Goals, capacity, and objectives
- **User Stories**: Detailed stories with acceptance criteria
- **Technical Tasks**: Breakdown by role and complexity
- **Sprint Ceremonies**: Meeting schedules and agendas
- **Risk Assessment**: Identified risks and mitigation strategies
- **Success Metrics**: Measurable outcomes and KPIs
- **Definition of Done**: Quality gates and completion criteria

## Sprint Planning Process

1. **Sprint Planning Meeting**: 2 hours at sprint start
2. **Daily Standups**: 15 minutes daily
3. **Sprint Review**: 1 hour at sprint end
4. **Sprint Retrospective**: 1 hour at sprint end

## Team Roles

- **Full-Stack Developer**: Backend and frontend development
- **UI/UX Designer**: Design system and user experience (part-time)
- **Product Owner**: Requirements and prioritization
- **Scrum Master**: Process facilitation and team coordination

## Sprint Metrics

- **Sprint Duration**: 2 weeks
- **Team Capacity**: 20 story points per sprint
- **Buffer**: 5% for unexpected issues
- **Velocity Tracking**: Story points completed per sprint
- **Quality Gates**: Code coverage, security review, user acceptance
